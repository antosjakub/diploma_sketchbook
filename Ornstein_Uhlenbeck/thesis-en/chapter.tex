\section{Formulation}


We consider the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (OU) process with anisotropic and correlated noises, 

$$
dX_t = -\tfrac{1}{2} x\,dt + \Sigma^\frac{1}{2} dW_t
$$

The Brownian noise is correlated with the covariance matrix $\Sigma\in\mathbb{R}^{d\times d}$
%, which is constructed as follows:
%- We generate random orthogonal matrix $Q$ from QR decomposition, serving as the eigenspace of $\Sigma$.
%- $\Sigma$'s eigenvalues $\Gamma = diag(\lambda_1, \lambda_2, · · · , \lambda_d)$ where $\lambda_{2i} \sim Uniform([1, 1.1])$ and $\lambda_{2i+1} = 1/\lambda_{2i}$.
%- Finally, $\Sigma = Q^T \Gamma Q$

We have
\begin{align}
\mu &= -\tfrac{1}{2} x \\ 
\sigma &= \Sigma^\frac{1}{2}
\end{align}

\begin{equation}

\partial_t p + \nabla\cdot J = 0
\end{equation}
