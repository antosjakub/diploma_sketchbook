\section{Formulation}

\begin{align}
\partial_t u - \alpha \Delta u &= 0 \\
u(x) &= u_D(x) \\
u(x,t) &= 0
\end{align}
where
$$
u(x) =
\prod_{i=1}^d \sin(\pi x_i)
+
0.67
\sum_{i=1}^d \sin(2\pi x_i) \left(\prod_{k=1,k\neq i}^d \sin(\pi x_k)\right)
$$


the corresponding analytical solution in then

$$
u(t,x) =
e^{-\gamma t}
\prod_{i=1}^d \sin(\pi x_i)
+
0.67
e^{-\delta t}
\sum_{i=1}^d \sin(2\pi x_i) \left(\prod_{k=1,k\neq i}^d \sin(\pi x_k)\right)
$$
where
\begin{itemize}
\item $\gamma = \alpha d \pi^2$
\item $\delta = \alpha (d+3) \pi^2$
\end{itemize}


Chosen so that it is slighlty more interesting then just a product of sines.
